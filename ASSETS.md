# Assets and sound references

The five masks in `src/main/resources/assets/nexvisuals/textures/particle/` are original procedural textures generated by `tools/generate_effect_textures.py` for NexVisuals. They and that generator are dedicated to the public domain under **CC0-1.0**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . No textures were extracted from another client or mod.

Hit Sounds references six sound events provided by the user's installed Minecraft resources:

| Preset | Minecraft sound event |
| --- | --- |
| Soft | `minecraft:block.note_block.harp` |
| Click | `minecraft:ui.button.click` |
| Pop | `minecraft:entity.chicken.egg` |
| Bell | `minecraft:block.note_block.bell` |
| Metallic | `minecraft:block.chain.hit` |
| Arcade | `minecraft:block.note_block.bit` |

**No Mojang audio, third-party audio, shader packs or reference-image artwork is distributed inside NexVisuals.** These are event references, not a claim of ownership or a license to redistribute Minecraft's assets. Resource packs may change the sounds heard by the user. Minecraft remains separately required.
